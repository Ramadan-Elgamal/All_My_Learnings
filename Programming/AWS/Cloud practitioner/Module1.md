> Each region contains at least 3 Availability Zones( AZ ) up to 6

>**Exam Tip:** This is one of the highest-yield topics on the exam. Memorize the rule: **AWS secures "of" the cloud (infrastructure); the customer secures "in" the cloud (their data and configuration).** If a scenario asks "who's responsible for X," map X to physical/infrastructure (AWS) or data/config/access (customer).

### CAF : Cloud Adaption Framework
#### Business perspective
- The Business Perspective in AWS CAF focuses on **aligning cloud investments with digital transformation goals and desired business outcomes.**
     - **Common stakeholders** include
        - Chief executive officer (CEO),
        - Chief financial officer (CFO),
        - Chief operations officer (COO)
        - Chief information officer (CIO)
        - Chief technology officer (CTO)
#### People perspective
- Emphasizing a culture of **continuous growth and learning** within organizations, emphasizing that **change is a normal and integral aspect of business operations.**
    - **Common stakeholders** include
        - CIO
        - COO
        - CTO
        - Cloud director,
        - Cross-functional
        - Enterprise-wide leaders.
#### Governance perspective
 - Orchestrate **cloud initiatives,** maximizing organizational benefits and **minimizing transformation-related risks.**
        - **Common stakeholders** include
            - Chief Transformation Officer
            - CIO
            - CTO
            - CFO
            - Chief data officer (CDO)
            - Chief Risk Officer (CRO).
#### Platform perspective
- Builds an **enterprise-grade, scalable, hybrid cloud platform.**
- Aids in **modernizing existing workloads.**
- Supports the **implementation of new cloud-native** solutions.
    - **Common stakeholders** include
        - CTO
        - Technology Leaders
        - Architects
        - Engineers
#### Security perspective
- Achieve the **confidentiality, integrity, and availability** of your data and cloud workloads
- **Common stakeholders** include
    - Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
    - Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
    - Internal Audit Leaders
    - Security Architects & Engineers
#### Operation perspective
- Ensure that your cloud services are **delivered at a level that meets the needs of your business.**
- **Common stakeholders** include
    - Infrastructure and Operations Leaders
    - Site Reliability Engineers
    - Information Technology Service Managers
    - Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

>**Exam Tip:** Remember: **Perspectives** = who's involved (Business, People, Governance, Platform, Security, Operations). **Domains** = what's transforming (Technology, Process, Organization, Product). **Phases** = when it happens (Envision, Align, Launch, Scale). The exam may test whether you can tell these three groupings apart.

>**Exam Tip:** If you see "Quick Starts" mentioned in older study material, know it's now the **AWS Solutions Library**. Similarly, **AWS Control Tower** (not "Landing Zone") is the current answer for automated multi-account setup with governance guardrails.

### **AWS Well-Architected Framework (WAF) by Pillar (6 total Sustainability was added in 2021):**
- **Operational Excellence:**_ Ensures efficient and effective operations, promoting automation and continuous improvement.
- _**Security:**_ Enhances the protection of information and systems, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- _**Reliability:**_ Improves system recovery and resilience, ensuring consistent performance and availability.
- _**Performance Efficiency:**_ Optimizes resource use, ensuring systems are scalable and adaptable to changing demands.
- _**Cost Optimization:**_ Reduces costs and maximizes value, ensuring financial efficiency in cloud usage.
- _**Sustainability:**_ Minimizes the environmental impact of running cloud workloads, e.g., through Region selection and efficient resource utilization.
